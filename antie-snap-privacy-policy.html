Effective date: November 11, 2026

AURSPARK ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Antie Snap (the "Game").

Important: This policy describes the Game as currently intended to operate without advertising, account registration, or online user tracking. If advertising, analytics, cloud services, or other third-party SDKs are added, this policy and the applicable app-store disclosures must be updated before release.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to play the Game.

The Game may store gameplay preferences and progress locally on your device, such as settings, scores, or other game-related information. This information is used to provide basic game functionality and is not transmitted to us by the Game as currently configured.

Your device or operating system may independently process technical information required to install and run the app. We do not use this information to identify you.

2. How We Use Information

Locally stored game information is used to remember your preferences and gameplay progress. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version of the Game is not intended to include third-party advertising or analytics services. Google Play and your device platform may process information under their own privacy policies when you download or use the Game.

If third-party services are introduced in a future version, we will identify the relevant services and explain their data practices in an updated policy.

4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

Game preferences and progress, where supported, are stored locally on your device. You can generally remove this locally stored information by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game, subject to your device's operating-system behavior.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. We take reasonable steps appropriate to the Game's design, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of apps and review the privacy settings available on their devices and app-store accounts.

6. Your Choices

You may stop using the Game at any time. You can uninstall it through your device's normal app-management settings. You may also clear locally stored app data through your device settings, although doing so may erase game progress.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features, services, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same location with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically.
