Privacy Policy

Slap! Face Off

Effective date: November 21, 2026
App version: 3.00

This Privacy Policy explains how AURSPARK ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Slap! Face Off mobile application (the "App"). It describes information processed by the App and by third-party services used to provide advertising and in-app purchases.

At a glance: Photos selected or captured for the 3D face feature are processed on your device by the App and are not uploaded to AURSPARK by that feature. The App also integrates Google Mobile Ads services. Those services may process device and advertising-related information as described below.

1. Information We Handle

  • Photos and camera: If you choose to select a photo or use the camera feature, the App accesses the image you select or capture to create the in-game 3D face effect. The App's face feature processes that image locally on your device; it is not uploaded to AURSPARK by this feature. Camera and photo access are optional.
  • Game settings and progress: The App may store settings and gameplay values locally on your device, such as your selected language, sound preference, weapon selection, and game scores. Certain weapon unlocks obtained by watching a rewarded advertisement are temporary and apply only to the current game session.
  • Advertising-related information: The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display Banner, Interstitial, App Open, and user-initiated Rewarded advertisements. In providing these services, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device identifiers or advertising identifiers, app and device information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, and consent or advertising preferences. The information processed and the purposes may depend on your device settings, applicable law, and the consent choices available to you.
  • Purchase information: If you purchase the optional "No Ads" product, Google Play processes the payment. The App may receive purchase status and transaction-related information, such as product identifiers, purchase tokens, or order references, as needed to grant or restore the relevant entitlement and protect against fraudulent transactions. We do not receive or store your full payment card number through the App.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: The App, Android, Google Play, and integrated service providers may process technical information needed to operate the App, deliver ads, maintain security, diagnose errors, and complete purchases.

2. How Information Is Used

Information described in this Policy may be used to:

  • Provide gameplay, the 3D face feature, local settings, and game progress;
  • Display and measure advertisements, including rewarded advertisements requested by you;
  • Process, verify, maintain, and restore an eligible No Ads purchase;
  • Maintain App security, prevent abuse or fraudulent transactions, and diagnose technical problems; and
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Advertising and Consent

The App uses Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Depending on availability, your region, device settings, and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Google may use cookies, mobile advertising identifiers, and similar technologies, and may process information for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

Where required, the App uses Google's consent tools to present privacy choices before requesting or displaying ads that require consent. You can review or change available advertising and privacy choices through the controls provided in the App (if available) and your Android or Google account settings. Changing a device advertising setting may not stop all advertising; it may affect personalization or measurement.

AdMob is provided by Google. Please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services for further details.

4. Rewarded Ads and No Ads Purchase

Rewarded advertisements are optional and are initiated by the user. When the required reward event is successfully completed and confirmed by the advertising SDK, the App may unlock the relevant special weapon for the current game session. Watching a rewarded ad does not create a permanent purchase or account entitlement.

The App may offer a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase called "No Ads". When recognized as owned, this purchase is intended to disable Banner, Interstitial, and App Open advertisements. It does not disable optional Rewarded advertisements that you choose to watch to unlock session-based weapons. Google Play handles payment processing and purchase records. Purchase restoration may be available through the App and is subject to Google Play account and purchase status.

5. Photos, Camera, and Sharing

Photo selection and camera use are optional. You decide whether to select an image or grant camera permission. Images used for the 3D face feature are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to AURSPARK by that feature. You can deny camera permission and continue using other game features. You can revoke camera permission in your Android device settings.

If you use your device's share function to share a screenshot or other content, the destination app or service you select may process that content under its own privacy policy. We do not control the destination service.

6. Sharing and Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may allow service providers to process information as necessary to provide the App's features, including:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): advertising delivery, consent-related functions, ad measurement, and fraud prevention; and
  • Google Play Billing: payment processing, purchase status, and purchase restoration.

These providers process information under their own privacy terms and may act as independent controllers for some processing. Their services may involve processing in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards.

The App may also request externally hosted resources, such as typefaces. When your device requests such resources, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and request details.

7. Local Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Game preferences and progress stored locally remain on your device until you clear the App's storage, reset the relevant data within the App (if available), or uninstall the App. Temporary image data used for gameplay is intended for in-session use and is not uploaded to AURSPARK by the 3D face feature.

We do not currently provide user accounts or maintain a user profile for ordinary gameplay. Advertising providers and Google Play may retain information according to their own policies and retention practices. Purchase records may also be retained by Google Play for accounting, legal, and transaction purposes.

To remove locally stored App data, use your Android device's app-storage settings or uninstall the App. For information held by Google, please use the privacy and account controls provided by Google.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the App's design to protect information under our control. The 3D face feature is designed to process selected images locally rather than upload them to our servers. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party providers are responsible for the security of information they process under their services.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly use the App to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the address below so we can review the request. Advertising and age-related settings may also be subject to Google Play and Google service controls.

You may also have choices about personalized advertising and consent. To make a privacy-related request to AURSPARK, contact service@aurspark.com. 11. International Processing

Third-party service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, such processing is subject to applicable legal safeguards and the provider's privacy commitments.
