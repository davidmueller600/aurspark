Privacy Policy

Symbol Adventure

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

Symbol Adventure (“the App”) is a casual puzzle game developed by AURSPARK (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the App, including when advertisements are displayed.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The App does not require you to create an account to play. Game settings, preferences, player profile selections, and gameplay records such as scores or achievements may be stored locally on your device to provide game features.

We do not ask you to provide your real name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location in order to play. If you choose to use a sharing feature, information or images you select to share will be handled by the sharing application or service you choose.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on the App version, ad availability, your region, consent status, and applicable settings, you may see the following formats:

Banner Ads Small advertising placements displayed within eligible App screens.
Interstitial Ads Full-screen advertisements that may appear at suitable transitions, such as after a completed game when you choose to restart or return to the home screen.
Rewarded Ads Optional video advertisements that you may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward, such as 15 additional seconds in a solo game. The reward is granted only when the required viewing and completion conditions are met.
App Open Ads Advertisements that may be shown when the App is opened or returns from the background, subject to ad availability, consent, cooldowns, and other display conditions.

When advertising is enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its partners may automatically collect or receive certain information to provide, deliver, measure, and improve advertisements, prevent fraud, and maintain service security. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, and your choices, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device’s general location;
  • advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers;
  • advertising and app interaction information, such as ad impressions, taps, video views, app launches, and related events;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash data, app launch time, and SDK performance; and
  • information about the device, operating system, language, and network connection.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements, measure advertising performance, detect invalid activity, and improve their services. Whether personalized advertising is available depends on applicable law, consent choices, and device or account settings.

For more information, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Consent and Advertising Privacy Choices

Where required, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent and privacy choices. The consent information may be refreshed when the App is launched. A consent message may be presented when required for your region or circumstances.

When Google’s consent configuration requires a privacy options entry point, the App provides an Ad Privacy Choices option in Settings & About. You can use it to review or change the choices made available by the consent form. This option may not be displayed in regions or situations where Google indicates that a privacy options entry point is not required.

If you decline personalized advertising where that choice is offered, you may still receive non-personalized or contextual advertisements. The choices available can depend on your region and applicable law. You may also be able to manage or reset your advertising identifier through your Android device settings or Google account settings.

4. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the App or its advertising providers may be used to:

  • operate gameplay features and remember local preferences;
  • display, deliver, and measure advertisements, where enabled;
  • provide rewards associated with optional rewarded advertisements;
  • maintain App stability and improve performance; and
  • detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party services, including Google AdMob and Google’s consent-management services. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the policies linked above. This Privacy Policy does not govern third-party services that we do not control.

6. Data Retention and Security

Gameplay information stored locally may remain on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to their respective retention practices and policies.

Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is protected in transit using encryption as described in Google’s documentation. We use reasonable measures appropriate to the App, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the App. Advertising and consent settings are configured in accordance with the audience selected for the App, applicable children’s privacy laws, and Google Play Families requirements where applicable. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

8. Your Choices

You can stop using the App at any time. You can generally remove locally stored App data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it, subject to your device’s operating system. You can manage available advertising privacy choices through the consent messages or the Ad Privacy Choices option when it is required and available, as well as through your device or Google account settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
