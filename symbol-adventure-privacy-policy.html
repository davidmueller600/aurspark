Privacy Policy

Symbol Adventure

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Symbol Adventure (“the App”) is a casual puzzle game developed by AURSPARK (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the App.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The App does not require you to create an account to play. Game settings, preferences, and gameplay records such as scores or achievements may be stored locally on your device to provide game features.

We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or precise location in order to play. If you choose to use a sharing feature, information or images you select to share will be handled by the sharing application or service you choose.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. When advertising is enabled in the version of the App you use, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its partners may automatically collect or receive certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertising, prevent fraud, and maintain service security.

Depending on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, and your choices, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers;
  • ad views, taps, video views, and other interactions with the App or advertisements;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash data, app launch time, and SDK performance; and
  • information about the device, operating system, and network connection.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements, measure advertising performance, detect invalid activity, and improve their services. The availability of personalized advertising depends on applicable law, consent choices, and device or account settings.

For more information, review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

3. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required, the App will use Google's consent tools, such as the User Messaging Platform (UMP), to request and manage advertising privacy choices. You may also be able to manage advertising preferences or reset your advertising ID through your device or Google account settings.

If you decline personalized advertising where that choice is offered, you may still receive non-personalized or contextual advertisements. The choices available can depend on your region and applicable law.

4. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the App or its advertising providers may be used to operate gameplay features and remember local preferences; display and measure advertisements, where enabled; maintain app stability and improve performance; and detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party services, including Google AdMob and Google's consent-management services. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review those policies. This Privacy Policy does not govern third-party services that we do not control.

6. Data Retention and Security

Gameplay information stored locally may remain on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to their retention practices and policies. We use reasonable measures appropriate to the App, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and consent settings must be configured in accordance with the audience selected for the App, applicable children's privacy laws, and Google Play Families requirements where applicable. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

8. Your Choices

You can stop using the App at any time. You can generally remove locally stored App data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it, subject to your device's operating system. You can manage available advertising preferences through consent choices presented in the App and your device or Google account settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
