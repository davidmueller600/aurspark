1. Introduction

Welcome to Countdown App, developed and published by AURSPARK ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Countdown App.

2. Information We Collect

Countdown App is designed to operate without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

We do not directly request personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

Countdown events and related information entered by users may be stored locally on their devices.

3. Data Storage

Information created within Countdown App, including countdown titles, dates, and preferences, may be stored locally on your device.

We do not operate our own server to collect or store this countdown information.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data may permanently remove locally stored information.

4. Device Permissions

Depending on the features available in the App, certain device permissions may be requested.

  • Notifications: Used to remind you of upcoming countdown events, if notification functionality is enabled.

You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

The App may use third-party services to support functionality, performance, analytics, or advertising if these services are integrated.

Such services may process certain technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Relevant third-party services will be disclosed in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

We do not share locally stored countdown information with third parties through our own servers.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Countdown information is retained locally on your device for as long as you keep it.

You can delete countdown information directly within the App, where supported, or remove locally stored application data through your device settings.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored application data.

8. Children's Privacy

Countdown App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been inadvertently collected from a child, please contact us.
