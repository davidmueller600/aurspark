Privacy Policy

App: Kitchen Rush
Developer: AurSpark
Last updated: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kitchen Rush (the “App”) handles information when you use the game. It applies to the current Android version of the App and describes the data practices of the game and its advertising services.

1. Information We Handle

Information you provide: The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to submit your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information to play.

Game data stored on your device: The App stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device. This may include coins, unlocked levels, stars, high scores, upgrades, boosters, sound and music settings, language, and gameplay preferences. This information is used to save and restore your game experience.

Device backup: If Android backup is enabled on your device, eligible local App data may be included in your device backup and restored by Android when you reinstall the App or move to another device. Backup availability and behavior are controlled by Android and your device/account settings.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Android version of Kitchen Rush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app open ads. Rewarded ads may provide in-game rewards when you choose to watch and successfully complete an eligible ad.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertising, analyze interactions, prevent fraud, and maintain SDK performance. Depending on the SDK version, device settings, and applicable law, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, ad impressions, taps, and video views; and
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash-related and SDK performance data.

Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's explanation of Google Mobile Ads SDK data collection.

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy and consent messages when required. Your available choices may depend on your location and applicable privacy requirements. The App checks the consent status before initializing or requesting ads, and it does not request ads unless the consent system indicates that ad requests are permitted.

Where a privacy options entry point is required or available, you can use the privacy choices option provided in the App to review or change your choices. You can also manage your device's advertising settings through Android settings. Changing device settings may affect advertising functionality or personalization.

For details about UMP, see Google's UMP documentation.

4. How Information Is Used and Shared

Local game data is used to maintain your progress and preferences. We do not operate an account system or a game-progress server for the features described in this policy.

Information collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted to Google and, where applicable, its advertising and measurement partners for ad delivery, ad measurement, analytics related to advertising, fraud prevention, and service operation. These third parties process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not currently integrate a separate third-party analytics or crash-reporting service into the App apart from the data processing that may occur through the Google advertising SDK.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove the data. Android device backup may retain eligible data according to your backup settings and Google's backup retention practices.

Advertising-related information is handled and retained by Google and its partners according to their policies and retention practices. We do not control the retention periods of information processed by those providers.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not require users to provide personal information directly to the developer. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services may process device and usage information as described above; advertising and consent settings are subject to applicable age-related requirements and platform settings. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps in the design and operation of the App to limit the information we handle. Local game data is stored using the App's device storage. Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads SDK is subject to Google's security practices, including encryption in transit as described in Google's documentation. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, advertising integrations, or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page. The “Last updated” date above indicates when this policy was most recently revised.
