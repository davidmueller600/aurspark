Privacy Policy

Slap! Face Off

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Slap! Face Off (“the App”) handles information when you use the game.

Important: This policy describes the current release as designed. Photos selected or captured for the 3D face feature are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers by the App. Recheck this statement if cloud services, advertising, analytics, or other network features are added.

1. Information We Collect

The current version does not require an account and is not designed to collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or payment information.

  • Photos and camera: If you select a photo or use the camera feature, the App accesses the image you select or capture to create the in-game 3D face effect. Processing is performed locally on your device. The App does not upload these photos to a server.
  • Game settings and progress: The App may store settings and gameplay values, such as your selected language, sound preference, player preferences, and game scores, locally on your device.
  • Technical information: The operating system or platform may process basic technical information when the App runs. The App does not intentionally use this information to identify you.

2. How We Use Information

Information available to the App is used to provide the game features you request, including displaying your selected photo as a 3D face, remembering local preferences, and maintaining gameplay state.

3. Photo and Camera Privacy

Photo access and camera use are optional. You decide whether to select an image or grant camera permission. Images used for the face feature are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted by the App to us. You can deny camera permission and continue using other game features. You can revoke camera permission in your device settings.

When you use your device’s share function to share a screenshot or other content, the destination app or service you select may process that content under its own privacy policy.

4. Local Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Game preferences and progress stored locally remain on your device until you clear the App’s storage, reset the relevant data within the App (if available), or uninstall the App. Temporary image data used during a game session is intended for in-session use and is not uploaded to our servers.

The current version does not provide user accounts or maintain a server-side user profile. To remove locally stored App data, use your Android device’s app-storage settings or uninstall the App.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. The current game is not designed to send your selected photos or gameplay preferences to us or to third parties.

The App may load typefaces or other resources from third-party providers, such as Google Fonts. When your device requests an externally hosted resource, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and request information. The provider handles that information under its own privacy terms.

If future versions add advertising, analytics, cloud processing, or other third-party SDKs, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declarations will be updated before those features are released.

6. Data Security

We design the current photo feature to process images locally rather than upload them to our servers. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Use your device’s security features to protect information stored on it.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not require an account or ask users to submit personal details. Parents or guardians with questions may contact the developer using the details below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same URL with a revised effective date.
